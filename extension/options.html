Chess Screen Coach settings

H key: Hide or show the entire on-page coach. While hidden, board monitoring and engine requests pause. This shortcut works on Chess.com when you are not typing in a field.

For flipped study boards, select a color explicitly. Screen orientation does not establish who you are. One Stockfish thread and 16 MiB hash. Longer searches may find better moves but use more CPU. Full strength is the default. Clear the checkbox to use the Elo slider. This changes the main agent, not the separate sparring opponent. Extra details are displayed on personal analysis and computer/practice pages.
